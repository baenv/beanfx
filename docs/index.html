beanfx demo

Two effects exported by beanfx, played by beanfx.js on a plain canvas: colour sheet, additive light sheet, states, mirror, hit-stop and screen shake from the JSON.

Use it in your game: copy beanfx.js next to the exported <name>.json and sheets, then createFx(await loadFx("fx/name.json"), {x, y}), fx.update(dt) and fx.draw(ctx) each frame. See porting.md in the skill for engines.
