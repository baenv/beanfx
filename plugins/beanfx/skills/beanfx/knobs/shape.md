# Carrier shape

What the moving part is and its silhouette at the peak: bolt, fan, beam, ground crawl, geyser, falling column,
claw, burst, ring, a creature.

**Fixed when** the prompt or an image describes the silhouette. A subject name alone ("fireball", "dragon") does
not fix it, and a word with two readings ("slash") is open.

**Ask** after the web research, so the options come from how the subject is known: "Which shape at the peak?" 2 to
4 silhouettes, each with an ASCII `preview` of the peak frame at about 16 x 8 characters and a line on what it
reads as at 1x (a fireball: round head with broken puffs, a comet teardrop, a spinning ring of flame). Recommend
the one that reads clearest at the chosen size. A reference game or art the owner names gives principles, never a
copy of its effect.

**Default:** the carrier the research and the element point to; 3 beats with a 1 to 2 frame peak.
